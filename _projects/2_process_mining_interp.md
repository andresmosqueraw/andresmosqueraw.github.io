**M.Sc. research at Universidad de los Andes (2025 to present).**

Process mining studies event logs: what happened, in what order, and why. I use it with machine learning, reinforcement learning, and LLMs for predictive and prescriptive process analytics.

A prescriptive recommendation comes from three models: a risk model, an effect model, and a timing model that decides when to act. My paper **RETIME** composes their explanations and tests them with deletion tests and path patching on BPIC2012 and BPIC2017. The effect model carries 56–81% of the decision's reasons. It is under review at ICPM.
