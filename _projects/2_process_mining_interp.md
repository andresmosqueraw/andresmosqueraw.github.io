**M.Sc. research at Universidad de los Andes (2025 to present).**

Process mining studies event logs: what happened, in what order, and why. I use it with machine learning, reinforcement learning, and LLMs for predictive and prescriptive process analytics.

I am now exploring the other direction: what process mining can say about the internal processes of language models, and how mechanistic interpretability can make process models more trustworthy.

Work in progress. Results will appear here.
