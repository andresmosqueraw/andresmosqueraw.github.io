Abstractive summaries of Indian court judgments (InLSum: 1,200 training judgments, 400 unseen test judgments). We compared GPT-4.1 prompting strategies, hybrid extractive and abstractive pipelines, and multi-agent state-graph architectures, evaluated with ROUGE-2, ROUGE-L, and BLEU. It placed 3rd on the official JUST-NLP 2025 leaderboard.
