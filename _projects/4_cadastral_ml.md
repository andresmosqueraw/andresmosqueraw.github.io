**Coplanin (2023–2025), machine learning engineer.**

- Trained a Pix2Pix GAN for cadastral image segmentation: 0.87 IoU and 92% accuracy, cutting manual rework by 60%.
- Fine-tuned Gemma-2, Llama 3, Mistral, Phi-4, and Qwen2.5 to split 57,000 Spanish names (81% accuracy) and infer gender (95%).
- Classified 35,000 addresses with 93% accuracy.

Stack: PyTorch, Hugging Face, Unsloth, OpenCV, PostgreSQL.
