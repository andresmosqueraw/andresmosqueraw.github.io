I'm a research M.Sc. student in Systems and Computing Engineering at **Universidad de los Andes**, on a full scholarship (Beca Avanza), working as a graduate research assistant. My research uses process mining together with machine learning, reinforcement learning, and LLMs, and I'm now connecting it to **mechanistic interpretability**.

In 2026 I won the **Global South AI Safety Hackathon** by Apart Research, competing solo (1 of 3 winning teams out of 217). The project used chain-of-thought attribution to find which reasoning steps push LLMs toward stereotypes about Latin America. In BlueDot Impact's **Technical AI Safety Project**, mentored by Jess Bergs (UK AI Security Institute), I extended it into a workshop paper. Now I'm facilitating a technical AI safety seminar for a cohort of 8 with **AI Safety Colombia**.

Before that, I spent two years as a machine learning engineer: GANs for land registry images, LLM fine-tuning for Spanish names, and MLOps at retail scale. I graduated first in my class in Software Engineering at Uniempresarial.

I'm looking for research collaborations and fellowships in interpretability, evaluations, and scalable oversight.
